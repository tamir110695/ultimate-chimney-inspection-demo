TOP OF THE FLUE

Chimney cap

A properly fitted cap helps keep rain, animals and debris out while allowing smoke to exit the flue.

◎

WHAT WE LOOK FORRust, torn mesh, loose fasteners and missing covers.

WATER PROTECTION

Chimney crown

The crown directs water away from the top of the masonry. Small cracks can grow through repeated freezing and thawing.

◎

WHAT WE LOOK FORHairline cracks, failed sealant and damaged concrete.

INSIDE THE STACK

Flue liner

The flue liner provides a passage for combustion gases and helps keep heat away from surrounding construction.

◎

WHAT WE LOOK FORCracked tiles, gaps, blockages and creosote buildup.

BRICK & MORTAR

Masonry stack

The brick and mortar form the chimney structure. Weathered joints and damaged brickwork can allow water inside.

◎

WHAT WE LOOK FORSpalling bricks, loose mortar joints and shifting masonry.
